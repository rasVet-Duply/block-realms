# Block Realms 3D

A premium, mobile-first 3D block puzzle game for the public, inspired by Block Blast. Players drag shapes onto a 10×10 board to clear rows and columns, build combos, and chase a best score.

Its signature is 20 hand-crafted worlds (Candy Kingdom, Ice Kingdom, Magma World, Cyberpunk City and more). Each one has its own 3D block shapes, materials, lighting, atmosphere, particles, sounds and clearing animation. Scores, settings and the chosen world are saved on the player's device.
